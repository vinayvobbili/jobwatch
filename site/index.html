What it does

Watch

Watches company boards

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Workday and more, plus HN's Who is hiring and VC portfolio boards. Fresh roles, real pay ranges, robots.txt respected.

Rank

A short, ranked daily digest

Only new roles that match you, optionally fit-scored against your resume: must-haves met, gaps, a score out of 100.

Apply

Apply queue and form fill

A short queue of jobs to apply to, with a resume tailored per job. The Chrome extension fills the form from your details and earlier answers.

Track

Tracking through to an offer

Every stage, the next step, follow-ups for applications that go quiet, a calendar feed, and offers side by side.

Prep

Interview prep

A prep sheet per interview, answers drafted from your own stories, a mock interview and a drill video.

Agents

An MCP server for assistants

An MCP server and an agent skill, so Claude and other assistants can run your search with you.

Private by design

  • It runs on your computer. jobwatch sends nothing home.
  • Your data stays on your machine. The only exception is a model you choose for fit scores and chat; on-device models send nothing anywhere.
  • You press Submit yourself. jobwatch finds and ranks. It never applies for you.
