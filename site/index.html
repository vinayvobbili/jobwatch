How it works

Six steps, each with a short video made from made-up data.

1

Set up

Install it with pipx, run jobwatch and follow the four onboarding steps. Your page opens on Today.

2

Find

jobwatch watches the company boards you pick. Today ranks the new roles, optionally with a fit score against your resume.

3

Apply

A short queue of jobs to apply to, with a resume tailored per job. The Chrome extension fills application forms from your details and earlier answers. You press Submit.

Chrome extension
4

Track

Every stage through to an offer, with the next step and a follow-up day. Status emails update it, and applications that go quiet get flagged.

5

Prep

A prep sheet per interview, built from the resume and answers you sent, with answers drafted from your own stories, a mock interview and a drill video.

6

Extras

Compare pay, career value and company health side by side, and see who you know at each company.

What it does

Watch

Watches company boards

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Workday and more, plus HN's Who is hiring and VC portfolio boards. Fresh roles, real pay ranges, robots.txt respected.

Rank

A short, ranked daily digest

Only new roles that match you, optionally fit-scored against your resume: must-haves met, gaps, a score out of 100.

Apply

Apply queue and form fill

A short queue of jobs to apply to, with a resume tailored per job. The Chrome extension fills the form from your details and earlier answers.

Track

Tracking through to an offer

Every stage, the next step, follow-ups for applications that go quiet, a calendar feed, and offers side by side.

Prep

Interview prep

A prep sheet per interview, answers drafted from your own stories, a mock interview and a drill video.

Agents

An MCP server for assistants

An MCP server and an agent skill, so Claude and other assistants can run your search with you.

Private by design

Where the AI runs: the Claude API, Antigravity or this Mac, the fallback chain, and what leaves your computer.

  • It runs on your computer. jobwatch sends nothing home.
  • Your data stays on your machine. The only exception is a model you choose for fit scores and chat; on-device models send nothing anywhere.
  • You press Submit yourself. jobwatch finds and ranks. It never applies for you.

On computers with 48 GB or more, 35B-A3B-style models (about 3B active parameters) are the fastest choice.

On an Apple Silicon Mac, use MLX: it's built for Apple's chips and runs faster than Ollama there. Ollama is for computers MLX doesn't run on.

Only the 16 GB row was tested: on a 16 GB Apple Silicon Mac it agreed with 28 of 29 expected verdicts, at 27 seconds a call and 7.5 GB of memory. The other rows are suggested, sized by memory. The Gemma 4 column is an alternative for each row; on Apple Silicon it is served the same way as the Qwen beside it.
