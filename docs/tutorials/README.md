# jobwatch tutorials

Short narrated videos, recorded from the real app on made-up data. Rebuild with `scripts/videos/make-videos`, then
`python scripts/videos/publish.py` (see `scripts/videos/README.md`). The files live in `site/tutorials/` and play on
the [home page](https://vinayvobbili.github.io/jobwatch/); this folder only lists them.

| Video | Length | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| `jobwatch-01-getting-started.mp4` | 1:11 | Install, the onboarding steps, Today |
| `jobwatch-02-daily-routine.mp4` | 1:12 | Today's picks and chips, details, the queue, I applied, applications, prep sheet |
| `jobwatch-03-where-the-ai-runs.mp4` | 0:59 | Claude API, Antigravity or this Mac; the fallback chain; what leaves your computer |
| `jobwatch-04-applying.mp4` | 1:11 | The apply queue, jobs found elsewhere, the extension filling a form, importing a LinkedIn list, companies to watch |
| `jobwatch-05-tracking.mp4` | 0:56 | Stages, follow-ups, applications that go quiet, Interviews, looking up a company, what you sent |
| `jobwatch-06-interview-prep.mp4` | 0:46 | Prep sheets, drafted answers from your stories, mock interview, drill video, find a story |
| `jobwatch-07-comps-and-contacts.mp4` | 0:42 | The Comps tab, who you know there, referrals |

Features added since these were recorded are in the [guide](../guide/README.md), not in a video: the ATS check, cover
letter and follow-up drafts, ranked interview questions, offices near you, the estimated pay, the Companies list's
All and None, the job details' stage picker, and the extension's popup panel and Draft reply.
